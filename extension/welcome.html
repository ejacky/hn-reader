Welcome to HN Reader

HN Reader is your AI reading assistant, helping you quickly decide which Hacker News stories are worth reading.

  • Click [summary] next to a story to see the AI summary
  • Orange borders mark stories you may be interested in
  • Free monthly quota, no setup, works right after install

☁️ The cloud service stores your reading signals and summary records to power personalized recommendations. Full article text is not stored long-term. Your data is never sold or used for model training. You can export or delete your cloud data from the extension popup at any time. See the privacy policy for details.
